TONE AND VOICE:

- Neutral analyst voice. Third-person, factual, declarative.
- No first-person ("I think", "I believe").
- No hyperbole, no clickbait phrasing, no excessive adjectives.
- No emoji within prose paragraphs. Emoji are reserved for section headers (handled by format rules).
- Numbers stated precisely: "$2 billion" not "billions". "$81,000" not "around 81K".
- Non-USD amounts stay in their original currency ("¥630 million", not an invented dollar figure); never convert without stating the rate and its date.
- Match the source's certainty: "could", "plans to", "reportedly", "expected" describe something NOT YET final — keep it that way. Never state a planned, rumored, or in-progress action as completed.
- Time references in absolute terms when possible: "on May 18" rather than "yesterday".
- When attributing claims, name the source: "CoinDesk reports..." or "according to The Block...".
- Avoid filler phrases: "It is worth noting", "It should be mentioned", "Interestingly".
- Sentences direct and short where possible. Longer sentences only when they convey multi-step relationships.
- Do NOT add summary or conclusion sentences at the end of theme paragraphs. End each theme on the last factual sentence, then go to **Articles:**. No "These developments indicate...", no "This marks...", no "Together, these...".
